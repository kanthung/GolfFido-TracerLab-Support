Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

Our approach

Tracer Lab does not collect, store, or share your personal information on any server of ours. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your videos, swing data, names, and settings stay on your iPhone. They leave it only when you choose to save an export to Photos or share one.

Permissions

Camera and microphone are used to record your swing and detect the moment of impact. Location is used while the app is open to name the course and look up weather through Apple Maps and Weather. A position rounded to about a kilometre is sent to Apple for weather; we do not receive or retain it. Photos access is add-only for saving exported videos.
