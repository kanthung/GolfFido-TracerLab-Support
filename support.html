Support

Help with recording, tracing, or sharing a swing.

For the best trace

Place the iPhone 2.5–7 metres behind the golfer in good light. Keep the ball near the on-screen guide and leave the phone still while recording.

If the ball is not found

Try brighter light, a clearer background, and a little more distance. Tracer Lab prefers to show no line rather than draw a misleading one.

Contact us

Email kanthung@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of the problem. Please do not send private swing videos unless needed.
